Launching Today

Help establish The 1884 Fund.

Today we launch the 1884 Legacy Circle to help establish The 1884 Fund, our emergency-relief fund for Round Rock fire families, currently under development.

1884

Why $18.84

The year it all began.

1884: the year Round Rock's first fire company formed. That's why the gift is $18.84 a month — the year it all began, given every month.

How It Adds Up

No one carries it alone.

Here's how it adds up: 100 people giving $18.84 each comes to $1,884 together, every month. No one carries it alone. Everyone carries a little, and together it becomes a steady source of help for the families behind Round Rock's firefighters.
